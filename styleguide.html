Texture & Surface Notes

Background: Warm off-white (#F8F7F5) — avoids the cold sterility of pure white.
Texture: Subtle grain/noise overlay (CSS or SVG filter) added to hero section for tactile feel — keeps it from looking too flat.
Cards: White surfaces with very soft drop shadows — depth without heaviness.
Accents: Color used sparingly — tags, hover states, gradient hero. White space does the heavy lifting.
Motion: All transitions 150–250ms ease. Cards lift on hover. Hero animates on load.
